# Workout Programming, Splits, and Weekly Volume

## Weekly Training Volume Benchmarks
Scientific research indicates that for optimal muscle protein synthesis and progressive strength gains:
- **Beginners (0-1 years lifting)**: 8 to 12 direct hard working sets per muscle group per week.
- **Intermediates (1-3 years lifting)**: 12 to 18 direct sets per muscle group per week.
- **Advanced (3+ years lifting)**: 16 to 22 direct sets per muscle group per week, often requiring periodized training blocks.

A "hard set" is defined as a set taken within 1 to 3 repetitions in reserve (RIR 1-3) of concentric muscular failure.

## Popular Workout Splits
Choosing an appropriate split depends heavily on weekly availability:

1. **Full Body Split (3 Days per Week)**:
   - Ideal for: Beginners, individuals with limited time (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
   - Advantage: Hits each major muscle group 3 times weekly, maximizing muscle protein synthesis frequency without excessive fatigue in any single session.

2. **Upper / Lower Split (4 Days per Week)**:
   - Ideal for: Intermediate athletes (e.g., Upper A on Monday, Lower A on Tuesday, Rest on Wednesday, Upper B on Thursday, Lower B on Friday).
   - Advantage: Optimal balance of frequency (2x weekly per muscle) and local recovery.

3. **Push / Pull / Legs (PPL) Split (3 to 6 Days per Week)**:
   - Push: Chest, Anterior/Lateral Deltoids, Triceps.
   - Pull: Latissimus Dorsi, Trapezius, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids, Biceps.
   - Legs: Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves, Abdominals.
   - Advantage: High specialization and volume per movement pattern with minimal overlap between consecutive training days.
