# Nutrition, Hydration, and Muscular Recovery

## Daily Macronutrient Targets for Athletes
1. **Protein**:
   - Optimal range for lean muscle hypertrophy and retention: 1.6 to 2.2 grams per kilogram of body weight (0.7 to 1.0 g/lb).
   - Distribute protein across 3 to 5 meals containing 0.4 g/kg of high-quality leucine-rich protein to trigger muscle protein synthesis (MPS).
2. **Carbohydrates**:
   - Primary energy substrate for high-intensity glycolytic strength training: 3 to 6 g/kg depending on training volume and daily output.
3. **Fats**:
   - Essential for endocrine function and joint lubrication: 0.8 to 1.2 g/kg (minimum 20% of total daily calories).

## Sleep and Central Nervous System (CNS) Regeneration
Muscle tissue repair and growth hormone secretion peak during Slow-Wave Sleep (Deep Sleep):
- Aim for 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted nightly sleep.
- Chronic sleep restriction (<6 hours) elevates systemic cortisol, decreases testosterone, blunts insulin sensitivity, and increases soft tissue injury susceptibility by up to 1.7x.
